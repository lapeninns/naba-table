# Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 29, 2026

This policy explains how Nab a Table collects, uses, and protects your information when you browse restaurants or
manage bookings.

## Information we collect

- Account details you provide (name, email address, phone number) when creating an account or confirming a
  reservation.
- Reservation details such as party size, date, time, preferences, and any notes you submit.
- Usage data like device type, browser, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from IP address.

## How we use your information

- Provide and manage bookings, confirmations, and guest communications.
- Improve our product, measure performance, and diagnose technical issues.
- Protect against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

## Sharing and disclosure

- We share booking details with the restaurant you select to fulfill your reservation.
- We use trusted service providers for hosting, email delivery, analytics, and support. They only process data to
  provide services on our behalf.
- We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the security of our platform.

## Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember preferences. We also use privacy-focused analytics to
understand product usage and improve performance.

## Data retention

We retain account and reservation data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services.
You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.

## Your choices

You can access, update, or delete your account information by signing in and managing your profile. If you need
help, please reach out via our contact page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form at `/contact`.
