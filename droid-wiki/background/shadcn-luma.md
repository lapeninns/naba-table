# Shadcn and Luma

The repo uses one shadcn/Radix primitive layer at `components/ui/**` and one Luma theme. `components.json`, `tailwind.config.js`, `scripts/check-no-shadcn.mjs`, and `scripts/check-luma-compliance.mjs` enforce that boundary.

Related: [Patterns and conventions](../how-to-contribute/patterns-and-conventions.md).
