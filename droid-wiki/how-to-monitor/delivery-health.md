# Delivery health

Delivery health is tracked through email/SMS delivery logs, provider webhooks, queue APIs, and ops dashboards. Key files include `src/app/app/(app)/email-delivery/page.tsx`, `src/app/app/(app)/sms-delivery/page.tsx`, `src/app/api/ops/email-delivery/**`, `src/app/api/ops/email-queue/route.ts`, `src/app/api/webhook/resend/route.ts`, and `src/app/api/webhook/twilio/sms-status/route.ts`.
